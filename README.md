## What it is

InfluenceFlow is one workspace for the whole agency job: the creators you scout, the brands you pitch, the emails in between, the deals you sign and the money that follows. It replaces the usual mix of spreadsheets, notes and a crowded inbox.

The core idea: **you work in Gmail as you always do — the CRM reads it.** Who you emailed, who answered, who went quiet, which follow-up is due, what price a creator quoted: it all lands on the right profile and moves the pipeline by itself.

It is a **private circle**. Every account is approved by hand, and each member has their own workspace that nobody else can see.

## Getting in

1. **Request access** — sign up with your email and a password on the [sign-up page](https://dhurimhalili.github.io/influenceflow-crm/#/signup).
2. **Wait for approval** — your request goes to the owner. Until it's approved you can't open the app.
3. **Check your inbox** — once approved, you get a "You're in" email with a one-time sign-in link.
4. **Log in** any time after that at the [login page](https://dhurimhalili.github.io/influenceflow-crm/#/login) with your email and password. Forgot it? Use **Forgot password** on the login page and a reset link is emailed to you.

The app works on phone, tablet and desktop, and can be installed to your home screen. It updates itself — an open tab picks up a new version within seconds, after saving your work.

## The daily loop

Open the **Dashboard** and work down it:

- **Follow up now** — people whose follow-up day has come. One tap lists them.
- **Reply needed** — they wrote last; it's your turn.
- **Cancel scheduled** — they replied while a follow-up was still waiting in Gmail's Scheduled folder. The CRM can cancel it for you (and does so on its own when the setting is on).
- **New thread** — a thread has had enough follow-ups; time for a fresh email.
- **Inbound leads** — brands and creators who emailed you first and aren't in the CRM yet. Each one says why it was picked; one click adds it.
- **Emails sent, replies, reply rate** — for today or any period, for everyone or just influencers or just brands, and per sending mailbox. Every number opens the list behind it.

A small coach tracks your daily outreach goal and nudges you about follow-ups that slipped.

## How Gmail keeps everything current

- **Status moves by itself.** Your email moves someone to *Contacted*, their answer to *Replied*, a first email from them to *Inbound*. A status you set by hand wins until a newer email arrives.
- **Real replies only.** Out-of-office and auto-replies never count. When a manager or agency answers for a creator, it's linked to that creator; a management "no" is shown as "Managed · DM them", not as a refusal.
- **Scheduled emails count as done.** An email waiting in Gmail's Scheduled folder counts as your follow-up, and disappears again if you cancel it.
- **Prices and media kits are read.** Rates a creator quotes by email are picked up with strict rules (and optional AI reading). Media kits and rate-card files are saved to the creator's profile.
- **Threads are respected.** Follow-ups stay in the right thread, and you're told when someone answers in an older one.
- **Every mailbox counts.** Several mailboxes per account; stats show which address sent what and which gets the replies.

## Pipeline

`New → Contacted → No reply → Replied → Negotiating → Roster / Signed`, plus **Inbound** for people who came to you and **Denied** for a clear no (with a "reach back" reminder a month later).

View it as a table, as cards or as a **board** — every stage has a column, with a strip of stage counts to jump straight to any of them.

## Influencers

- **YouTube numbers filled in for you** — channel name, country, niche, subscribers, average views of the latest long videos (Shorts and streams left out, highest and lowest dropped) and engagement. Refreshed every hour; anything you edit by hand is kept, and **Refresh YouTube** takes YouTube's values back when you want them.
- **Scorecard** — your own star ratings for audience, niche fit, engagement and consistency, weighted the way you choose.
- **Rates and negotiation** — every format (integration, dedicated, Shorts, streams, add-ons) with list price, your agreed price and full history with undo.
- **Duplicates caught** — two records with the same email, profile or YouTube channel get a red alert and are pinned to the top until you merge them (all emails, files and history move into the one you keep) or mark them as different people.
- **Contact info at a glance** — creators you can't reach at all are marked, and team members (managers, agents) are linked from their emails.
- **Media kit page** — publish a clean public page for a creator to send to brands, with only verified numbers.

## Brands

- **Briefs** — what each brand wants: niches, platforms, creator size, formats, budget, markets, audience and what to avoid. **Best-fit creators** ranks your roster against it.
- **People** — several contacts per brand, each with their own email history.
- **Email composer** — drafts and templates per brand, sent through your Gmail.

## Deals and money

- **Campaigns** link a brand to its creators, with every common deal model (flat fee, CPM, hybrid, revenue share, retainer, usage rights and more). Your margin and the creator's payout are worked out for you.
- **Invoices** are chased on your follow-up schedule; a **contract** is drafted from the deal terms.
- **Pitch PDF** — tick creators and get a brand-ready shortlist with your prices, never their cost.

## Reports

- Reply rates, reply speed, which follow-up gets the answers, the best time to email, results by niche, country and mailbox, and revenue and profit from agreed deals only.
- A **weekly report email** lands in your inbox on Monday morning (or Sunday evening) in your own time zone. You can preview it inside the app.

## Import and export

Import from any spreadsheet (see above). Export to real Excel files, CSV, JSON, a table you can paste into Docs or Sheets, or a printable PDF — everything at once from Settings.

## Privacy and security

- **Your workspace is yours alone.** Every table is locked to its owner at the database level, and only approved members can read anything at all.
- **Gmail access is limited and encrypted.** For your CRM contacts the CRM keeps who, when, the subject and Gmail's short preview — not your inbox. Google's access tokens are encrypted at rest.
- **Hardened app.** Strict content security policy, protection against being framed by other sites, and server-side checks on every action. The app and its backend are security-scanned.
- **Nothing is sent from your outreach mailboxes without you.** Account emails (approval, password reset) come from the CRM's own address.

## For the owner: Members

**Members** (owner only) lists everyone who asked to join.

- **Approve** someone and they get their welcome email automatically.
- **Remove access** signs them out everywhere but keeps their data, so they can be restored later.
- **Delete** removes the account and everything in it, files included. You confirm by typing their email.
- **Password reset** sends a reset email, or gives you a link to send yourself.

---

## Questions

**Can other members see my creators or deals?** No. Each workspace is private. Teams only ever share names and daily outreach counts, and only if you opt in.

**Do I have to use Gmail?** No — you can log calls, DMs and WhatsApp messages by hand and the follow-up rules still work. Gmail just saves you the typing.

**What happens to my data if I leave?** Export everything from Settings at any time. When an account is deleted, its data is deleted with it.
